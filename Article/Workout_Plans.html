Train anywhere. Add weight when you're ready.

Pick your equipment level and the emphasis you want. Each plan is three sessions a week, with a rest day between.

Plan emphasis

Warm-up (5–8 min)

Easy marching or jumping jacks, arm and hip circles, 10 bodyweight squats, 10 cat-cows. Then do one light set of your first exercise.

Rest between sets

60–90 seconds for most exercises. Take 2–3 minutes after heavy barbell lifts.

Cool-down (5 min)

Stretch quads, hamstrings, chest and hip flexors for 30 seconds each side. Breathe slowly.

Weekly rhythm

Mon / Wed / Fri, or any three days with a rest day between. Walk 20–30 minutes on the other days.

General fitness guidance, not medical advice. If you have an injury, a health condition, or are pregnant, check with a doctor before starting. Stop any exercise that causes sharp pain. Both emphasis options work for anyone: choose the one that matches your goal.
